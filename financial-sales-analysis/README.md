# Financial Sales Analysis

## Overview
Analysis of 2,005 financial sales orders for a multinational 
retail company to identify revenue drivers, top performers, 
and profitability patterns across regions and product categories.

## Tools Used
Excel · Python · pandas · matplotlib · Power BI

## Business Questions Answered
1. Which region generates the highest revenue?
2. Who are the top 5 sales reps?
3. Which product category is most profitable?
4. How does revenue trend month over month?
5. Which customer segment drives the most sales?
6. What is the impact of discounts on profit?
7. Which payment method dominates high-value orders?
8. Which quarter consistently underperforms?

## Key Findings
- North America leads with $2.25M in total sales
- Software has the highest profit margin at 58.47%
- Fatima Al-Hassan is the top sales rep at $0.81M
- Q4 is the strongest quarter consistently
- SMB segment generates the most sales volume
- Overall profit margin is 40.05%
