# Project 03 — E-Commerce Sales Analysis 2010-2011

## Overview
Analysis of 541909 transactions from a UK-based 
e-commerce store to identify revenue drivers, top products, 
and seasonal trends.

## Business Questions Answered
- Which country generates the most revenue - UK
- What are the top selling products - Paper Craft, Litle Birdie
- How does revenue trend month by month - November has the most revenue- Christmas
- What percentage of revenue comes from the UK - 82.01%

## Key Findings
- UK represents 82.01% of total revenue
- Paper Craft, Little Birdie is the best selling product
- Revenue peaks in November driven by Christmas seasonality
- 4347 unique customers across 37 countries
