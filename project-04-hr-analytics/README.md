# Project 04 — HR Analytics
## Overview
Analysis of 1,470 employee records to identify attrition
drivers and provide recommendations to reduce employee turnover.

## Business Questions Answered
- What is the overall attrition rate? — 16.1%

- Which department loses the most employees? — Research & Development (133 departures)

- Does overtime affect attrition? — Yes, overtime workers are significantly more likely to leave.

- Which job roles have highest attrition? — Laboratory Technician and Sales Executive.

- How does age relate to attrition? — Younger employees (Avg. age 33) leave more frequently than older staff.

## Key Findings
- The Overtime Connection: Employees working overtime account for 53.6% of total attrition, despite making up a smaller portion of the overall workforce.

- Departmental Volume: The Research & Development department experienced the highest loss with 133 employees leaving, followed by Sales with 92 departures.

- Age Demographics: There is a clear age gap in retention; the average age of those who left is 33.6 years, while those who stayed average 37.5 years.
