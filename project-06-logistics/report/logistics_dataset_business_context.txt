
Business Context:
This dataset represents a multinational logistics and delivery company handling freight operations
across multiple countries. The company manages different freight modes including air, sea, road,
and rail shipments. Orders vary by booking priority, delivery timelines, and operational performance.
The dataset includes realistic operational issues such as delayed deliveries, cancellations,
missing operational data, duplicate records, and extreme revenue outliers, making it suitable
for real-world analytics, Power BI dashboards, KPI reporting, and data cleaning projects.

Suggested Analysis Questions:
1. Which freight type generates the highest total revenue?
2. Which cities and countries contribute the most orders?
3. What is the percentage of on-time deliveries by freight type?
4. Which drivers handle the most successful deliveries?
5. How do express bookings impact transit days?
6. What factors contribute most to delivery delays?
7. Which months recorded the highest logistics revenue?
8. Are there operational anomalies or outliers affecting business performance?
