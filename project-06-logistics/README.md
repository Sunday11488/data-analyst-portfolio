
# Project 06 — Logistics Management Analytics

## Overview
This project analyzes a realistic, AI-generated logistics orders dataset for a multinational freight operator. The analysis examines revenue drivers, delivery performance, and operational anomalies to surface actionable insights for operations and commercial teams.

## What Makes This Different
The dataset (959 clean orders) was generated and augmented using AI to simulate realistic logistics behaviour, including delays, cancellations, and revenue outliers for robust testing.

## Business Questions Answered
1. Which freight type generates the highest total revenue?
2. Which cities and countries contribute the most orders?
3. What is the percentage of on-time deliveries by freight type?
4. Which drivers handle the most successful deliveries?
5. How do express bookings impact transit days?
6. What factors contribute most to delivery delays?
7. Which months recorded the highest logistics revenue?
8. Are there operational anomalies or outliers affecting business performance?

## Key Findings
- **Total revenue (all freight types)**: £13,460,000 (£13.46M)
- **Top revenue freight type:** Sea freight generated the most revenue — £8,000,000.
- **Total orders:** 959 total orders in the cleaned dataset.
- **Busiest city:** Abuja is the busiest city by order count.
- **On-time delivery performance:** Overall on-time rate is 67.15%.

## Tools Used
Python · pandas · matplotlib · Power BI · AI (ChatGPT - for dataset)

## Power BI Dashboard
The dashboard is two pages:
- **Executive Overview:** KPI cards showing Total orders (959), Sea freight revenue (£8,000,000), and Overall on-time rate (67.15%), plus a freight-type revenue breakdown and a monthly revenue trend chart.
- **Operational Insights:** Interactive map of routes and volumes, driver performance heatmap, on-time rate by freight type, transit time distribution, and filters for freight type, booking priority, and month.
