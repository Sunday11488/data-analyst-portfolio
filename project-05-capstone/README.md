# Project 05 — SamiCommerce UK Sales & Customer Analysis (Capstone)

## Overview
Capstone project analyzing 397,924 clean transactions from 
SamiCommerce UK to deliver a full business intelligence report 
covering revenue analysis, customer segmentation, churn risk, 
and strategic recommendations.

## Business Questions Answered
- Which countries generate the most revenue?
- Who are our most valuable customers and what do they buy?
- Which customer segments should we prioritize for retention?
- What is monthly bases revenue growth rate?
- Which products are driving losses, and why? 
- Which product are generating the most revenue? 
- Which customers have not purchased in the last 3 months and are at risk of churning?


## Key Findings
- UK represents 82.01% of total revenue
- Paper Craft, Little Birdie is the best selling product
- Revenue peaks in November driven by Christmas seasonality
- 1449 customers are at the risk of churning

## Recommendations
- Allocate dedicated marketing budget to Netherlands, Germany, 
and France which show growth potential.
- Front-load inventory and launch promotions 
in October to capture early buyers and extend the peak period.
- Implement a loyalty program 
specifically for this segment — losing one Champion costs 
more than losing 10 casual buyers.
- A targeted email 
campaign with a discount offer could recover even 10% of 
these — potentially £300K+ in recovered revenue.
