# Business Problem

## Context
I am preparing this analysis for the e-commerce sales manager at SamiCommerce UK, a UK-based retailer serving multiple international markets. The goal is to turn transactional and customer data into strategic insights for sales growth and more profitable inventory decisions.

## Problem Statement
- Which countries generate the most revenue
- Who are our most valuable customers and what do they buy?
- Which customer segments should we prioritize for retention?
- What is monthly bases revenue growth rate?
- Which products are driving losses, and why?
- Which product are generating the most revenue?
- Which customers have not purchased in the last 3 months and are at risk of churning?

## Why It Matters
Without these answers, SamiCommerce UK risks making decisions based on incomplete information: overstocking low-demand inventory, under-investing in high-value customers, and failing to act quickly when growth slows. 

## Approach
- SQL: extract and aggregate sales, customer, and product data from the transaction database.
- Python / pandas : Use to annalyse and get the problem stated 
- Tableau: build visual dashboards to communicate python findings
