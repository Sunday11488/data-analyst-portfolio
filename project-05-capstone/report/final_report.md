# SamiCommerce UK — Final Analysis Report

## Executive Summary
Analysis of 397,924 clean transactions from SamiCommerce UK 
revealed critical insights about revenue concentration, seasonal 
patterns, customer behavior, and churn risk that directly impact 
the business growth strategy.

## Key Findings

### 1. Revenue Concentration
The UK generates £7.3M representing 82.01% of total revenue.
The next largest market, Netherlands, generates only £285K.
This represents dangerous geographic concentration.

### 2. Seasonal Performance
Revenue peaked in November at £1.1M.
September to November showed the strongest growth period.
December shows a sharp drop due to incomplete data.

### 3. Customer Segmentation
Of 4,339 total customers:
- Champions: 948 (most valuable, recent, frequent buyers)
- Potential: 1,908 (growing customers to nurture)
- Lost: 1,208 (haven't purchased in long time)
- At Risk: 275 (showing signs of disengagement)

### 4. Churn Risk
33.4% of customers (churned) have not purchased in 90+ days.
Immediate re-engagement campaigns are critical.

## Recommendations

1. **Reduce Geographic Risk:** The UK represents 82% of revenue — 
a single market disruption could devastate the business. 
Allocate dedicated marketing budget to Netherlands, Germany, 
and France which show growth potential.

2. **Maximize Q4 Revenue:** November peaks at £1.1M driven by 
Christmas demand. Front-load inventory and launch promotions 
in October to capture early buyers and extend the peak period.

3. **Protect Champion Customers:** 948 Champions drive 
disproportionate revenue. Implement a loyalty program 
specifically for this segment — losing one Champion costs 
more than losing 10 casual buyers.

4. **Launch Churn Re-engagement Campaign:** 1,449 customers 
(33.4%) haven't purchased in 90+ days. A targeted email 
campaign with a discount offer could recover even 10% of 
these — potentially £300K+ in recovered revenue.
